# Product requirements

The MVP supports one authenticated merchant, one store, and one primary product.
It must complete generation, controlled editing, immutable publishing, public SSR,
validated behavior tracking, experimentation, analytics, and merchant-confirmed
variant publication. Phases and algorithms are authoritative in `docs/phases.md`.

Payments, orders, purchases, revenue, inventory, shipping, CRM, platform migration,
custom domains, multiple themes, and autonomous publishing are excluded.
